Process

How a process can look

There isn't one fixed process — it depends on whether what you need already exists. Two common routes:

Route A · most common

Seats on existing courses

What you need is already in the catalogue. We size the cohort, assign seats and you're running — often inside two weeks.

Route B

A course built around your case

Nothing off the shelf fits. Our learning designers build with your subject experts, or adapt an existing course to your procedures. Longer — think two to three months.

  1. 01

    First contact

    You send the form or call. We reply within one working day.

  2. 02

    Needs mapping

    A 45-minute session: roles, gaps, timeline and budget.

  3. 03

    Programme & offer

    We shortlist or scope, size the cohort and send a written offer.

  4. 04

    Rollout

    Seats assigned, participants onboarded, your admin trained.

  5. 05

    Follow-up

    Progress reporting through the programme and a close-out review.

How we went forward with Odontia

A dental group with clinics across the country, all needing the same updated certification.

  1. 01
    They called in March. Their clinic managers had flagged that certification was drifting out of date across sites.
  2. 02
    We mapped it in one session. 38 people, four job roles, a hard deadline before the autumn inspection round.
  3. 03
    Route A, mostly. Three existing courses covered it; one module was adapted to their own procedures. Offer out in nine days.
  4. 04
    Rolled out per clinic, not all at once, so no site lost coverage. Their operations lead ran licence assignment herself.
  5. 05
    Completion reported monthly to the clinic managers. All 38 finished before the deadline.
