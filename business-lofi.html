BIO-midler

County-administered funding for competence development in existing businesses facing restructuring.

Typically covers
A share of course cost and lost working hours.
Who qualifies
Businesses in the county, subject to that county's current criteria.
Applied for by
The employer. We supply programme documentation.

Kompetansepluss

National scheme for basic skills training in the workplace — reading, writing, numeracy, digital and Norwegian.

Typically covers
Course delivery for eligible basic-skills training.
Who qualifies
Employers with staff who need basic skills, via an approved provider.
Applied for by
